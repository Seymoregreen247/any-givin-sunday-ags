Simple Rules

How to Play

1
Pick one winner in every game.
Tap the team you think will win.
2
Submit before kickoff.
Each game locks when that game begins.
3
One correct pick = one point.
No point spread is required in the standard game.
4
Highest score wins the week.
Correct percentage = correct picks ÷ completed games.
5
Highest correct percentage leads.
Example: 10 correct out of 15 completed games = 66.7%.
6
Ties go to the tiebreaker.
Closest predicted combined score for the final game wins the tie.
AGS House Rules

Friendly Competition. Clear Rules.

✓
Free to play in the app.
AGS does not collect entry fees, accept wagers, hold balances, or distribute winnings.
✓
Picks are for fun and bragging rights.
Standings, percentages, printable sheets and group discussion are the core experience.
✓
One pick per game.
Straight Pick'em is the default: choose the team you think will win.
✓
Games lock at kickoff.
Once a game's scheduled kickoff time arrives, that matchup can no longer be changed.
✓
Late schedule changes can be updated by Admin.
Kickoff times and weekly schedules should be refreshed before the pool opens.
✓
Football lines are informational only.
If favorites, spreads or totals are shown later, AGS will display them as reference information and not as a wagering interface.
Important: AGS Football Pool is designed as a free prediction and scorekeeping game. It is not a sportsbook and does not process bets, entry fees, stakes, prizes of value, deposits or payouts. Local laws can vary, so any separate money activity should be reviewed independently before use.
